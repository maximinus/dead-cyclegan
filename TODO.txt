Future Work
-----------

The projects goal is to convert GD audience recordings to GD soundboard recordings with machine learning.

Concrete results so far are:

* We are able to use raw audio in tensorflow.
* We have a discriminator that can detect sbd vs aud.
* We have a working implementation of Cyclegan for images.
* We have the same cyclegan for audio, and it can output audio.

Current issues:

* The sound returned is centered around -1 when viewed in Audacity. What should it be?
* What direction should the graphs move by? What could push them in that direction?
* What does the graph of the discriminator by itself look like?
* Once this is established, is that the same direction the discriminator looks like in the cyclegan?


Possible methods to improve:

* Impairing the discriminator by randomly giving false labels to real images (one-sided label flipping)
* Impairing the discriminator by adding dropout layers
* Improving the generator's ability to create more features by increasing the number of filters in its convolution layers
* Impairing the discriminator by reducing its number of filters
